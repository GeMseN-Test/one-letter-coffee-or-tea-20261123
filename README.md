## The answer: A — coffee

The program writes exactly one letter, `A`, to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else.
No word, no explanation, no punctuation — just the letter.

## Why coffee, not tea

- **Vivid:** coffee is hot, bitter, aromatic — you feel it the moment it lands. Tea is gentle; coffee wakes you up.
- **Funny:** "don't talk to me before my first coffee" is a rule millions of people genuinely live by. Nobody ever said that about tea.
- **True:** coffee is the morning ritual, bitter but honest — the fuel that turns a person into a functioning human before work starts. Tea waits patiently; coffee gets up and works.
- **First:** `A` is the first letter, and coffee is the first cup of the day.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single character `A` (UTF-8, no newline) to `/output/entry.txt`.
The output is deterministic — verified by running twice and comparing sha256 hashes (identical).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt  # A
```
